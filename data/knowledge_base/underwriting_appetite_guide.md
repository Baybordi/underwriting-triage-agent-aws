# SME Property Underwriting Appetite Guide

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This guide sets out the underwriting appetite for Small and Medium-sized Enterprise (SME) commercial property insurance. It applies to standard property risks with a total sum insured up to £5,000,000. Risks outside this scope must be referred to a senior underwriter.

## 2. Accepted Business Types
We actively insure the following SME property risks: offices, retail shops, cafés and restaurants, bakeries, small hotels and guest houses, warehouses used for general storage, light manufacturing units, hair and beauty salons, and professional service premises. These business types fall within standard appetite when all other rules in this guide are met.

## 3. Declined Business Types
We do not insure the following business types under any circumstances: fireworks manufacturing or storage, explosives handling, chemical plants, oil and gas refineries, waste and recycling plants with open burning, nightclubs with pyrotechnics, and any business operating illegally. Submissions matching these types must be declined.

## 4. Referred Business Types
The following business types are outside standard appetite and must be referred to a senior underwriter rather than quoted automatically: large-scale heavy manufacturing, businesses handling hazardous materials, listed or heritage buildings, properties with a sum insured above £5,000,000, and any business combining a high-hazard activity (for example a commercial kitchen) with a high-value building.

## 5. Geographic Appetite
We insure properties located in the United Kingdom only. Properties in designated high flood-risk zones are referred to a senior underwriter. Properties outside the UK are declined.

## 6. Property Condition Rules
Buildings must be in good structural condition and in active use. Buildings constructed before 1920 are referred to a senior underwriter due to age-related risk. Buildings that are vacant, derelict, or undergoing major structural works are referred, not quoted automatically.

## 7. Fire Protection Requirements
Properties must have working fire detection appropriate to the business. Cafés, restaurants, bakeries, and any premises with a commercial kitchen must have both a fire alarm and a fire suppression or sprinkler system. A submission from a business with a commercial kitchen and no suppression system must be referred.
