# SME Property Indicative Pricing Guidance

## 1. Status of This Guidance
The premium ranges in this document are indicative only. They are intended to give the underwriter a starting point. The agent must never present a price as final or binding. The underwriter sets and approves the final premium.

## 2. Risk Level Definitions
A risk is Low when it is a standard accepted business type, the building was constructed in 1920 or later, the claims history is clean, and all fire protection requirements are met. A risk is Medium when it is within appetite but has one elevating factor, for example two claims in five years or a commercial kitchen. A risk is High when it has multiple elevating factors but is still within appetite and not a referral or decline.

## 3. Indicative Premium Bands
For a Low risk, the indicative annual premium is approximately 0.15% to 0.25% of the sum insured. For a Medium risk, the indicative annual premium is approximately 0.25% to 0.40% of the sum insured. For a High risk, the indicative annual premium is approximately 0.40% to 0.60% of the sum insured.

## 4. Worked Example
For a Low-risk office with a sum insured of £1,000,000, the indicative annual premium range is approximately £1,500 to £2,500. This is guidance for the underwriter and is not a quote.

## 5. Pricing Exclusions
No indicative price should be produced for any risk that must be declined or referred. For declined risks, the output is the decline reason only. For referred risks, the output is the referral reason only, with pricing left to the senior underwriter.
